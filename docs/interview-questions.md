# Interview questions

For Karl. Read these aloud. 45 minutes. Record the screen. Do not point, and do not say “copy,” “calendar,” “network,” or “Adventure Anywhere” before they do.

Recruit from this club’s list. Skip anyone who builds the site.

- **Members (5).** Tried to join a Catholic Mountain Club trip in the last year, or opened one and stopped. At least 2 finished a signup. At least 2 did not. At least 3 use a phone as their main screen. Session on their phone. Start at https://cmc.adventureanywhere.org/trip/nov-second-saturday
- **Leaders (4).** Posted a trip on this host in the last year. Include one person who posted their first trip recently. Laptop, then the same tasks on their phone. If they will not save a new trip, reopen a trip they already posted and walk the form without saving.

## Opener (both)

1. “Thanks for doing this. I’ll ask you to try a few things on the site and talk out loud. There is no right way. I won’t help unless you’re stuck and want to stop. Is it okay if I record the screen?”
2. “When did you last try to join a trip?” (member) or “When did you last post a trip?” (leader)
3. “What were you trying to do that day?”

Write their words. Do not correct them.

## Member tasks

Start logged in or logged out the way they normally would. If they are not a member of this club, stay logged out.

**M1.** “Get as far as you can toward signing up for this hike. Stop before you commit if you don’t want to actually sign up. Tell me when you would stop in real life.”

Watch: Register, Thinking about it, Wrong time for me, login, and whether they can say if they can go, where to meet, and whether a ride is open.

At a pause: “What are you looking for?”

**M2.** “Find a different trip this club is running. Open it.”

Watch: left list, Calendar View, Back, or giving up. On a phone, watch whether they can reach the list.

**M3.** “See what another club on this network has coming up. Open one trip.”

Watch: the globe, leaving this host, or not finding other clubs.

## Leader tasks

**L1.** “Make a trip like this one, the way you normally would. Use a date you can throw away. Stop before you save if you don’t want a real trip posted.”

Watch: copy, the long form, the blank trip URL, organizer, waiver, times, capacity, and whether they look for the route or the meeting place and cannot find them.

At a pause: “What are you looking for?”

**L2.** “Show me how someone else would know this trip exists.”

Watch: announce, the left list, Calendar View.

**L3.** “Show me how a person would get a seat in a car, and how you would see who is coming.”

Watch: carpool, Take Seat, invite, Pending, Waitlisted.

**L4.** On the phone: “Start the same new trip again, and stop when the save button is hard to reach or you would quit.”

## If they stall

- “What are you looking for?”
- “What did you expect that to do?”
- “Keep going however you would at home.”

Do not name the control. If they ask you to take over, mark the task incomplete and go to the next one.

## Last five minutes (opinion)

Ask only after the tasks. Tag every answer here as opinion.

1. “Where did you pause or almost quit?”
2. “What do you use when this gets frustrating? A text, a person, another site?”
3. “What would you change?”
4. “Anything else about joining?” (member) or “Anything else about posting a trip?” (leader)

Do not ask whether it looks old, whether the buttons are confusing, whether the phone layout is broken, or whether switching trips is hard.

## Notes

For each task: task id, URL, logged in or not, phone or laptop, finished or stopped, the words they used, and a screenshot of each wrong turn.

- **Friction:** a pause, the wrong control, Back, a question to you, or quitting.
- **Opinion:** a wish with no stall.

A change goes into the design only when the same friction shows up for 2 people, or for 1 person and the matching row on the issue list.
